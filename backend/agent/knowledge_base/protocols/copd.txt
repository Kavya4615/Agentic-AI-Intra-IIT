# COPD Exacerbation Protocol (GOLD Guidelines summary)

1. SEVERITY ASSESSMENT
- Assess severity using ABG: mild (no acidosis), moderate (pH 7.25-7.35), severe (pH < 7.25).
- SpO2 target: 88-92% in COPD patients (avoid hyperoxia which can suppress hypoxic drive).

2. PHARMACOLOGICAL MANAGEMENT
- Administer short-acting bronchodilators (SABA + SAMA nebulisation) immediately.
- Give systemic corticosteroids (prednisolone 40mg/day for 5 days).
- Use antibiotics if sputum purulence or clinical signs of infection are present.

3. RESPIRATORY SUPPORT
- Controlled oxygen therapy: titrate to target SpO2 88-92%.
- Non-invasive positive pressure ventilation (NIPPV/BiPAP) for moderate-severe exacerbations with respiratory acidosis (pH < 7.35, PaCO2 > 45 mmHg).
- Intubation and mechanical ventilation if NIPPV fails or is contraindicated.

4. MONITORING
- Monitor respiratory rate continuously. Rising RR (>24/min) with worsening SpO2 indicates need for escalation.
- Watch for paradoxical breathing and accessory muscle use.
