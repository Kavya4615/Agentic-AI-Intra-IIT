# Sepsis Protocol (Surviving Sepsis Campaign Guidelines summary)
1. INITIAL RESUSCITATION
- Begin administration of crystalloids at 30 mL/kg for hypotension or lactate >= 4 mmol/L.
- Apply vasopressors if blood pressure is not restored (target MAP >= 65 mm Hg).

2. INFECTION MANAGEMENT
- Administer broad-spectrum intravenous antimicrobials within 1 hour of recognition.
- Obtain blood cultures before administering antimicrobials.

3. HEMODYNAMIC SUPPORT
- Use norepinephrine as the first-line vasopressor.
- Continuously monitor heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation. Note that rapid heart rate (tachycardia) combined with low blood pressure is a strong indicator of septic shock.
